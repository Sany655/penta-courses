## 3. Core Principles

1. **Outcome-Driven Value:** Every product is packaged around defensible, concrete outcomes (e.g. *"Build a working understanding of Clinical ABG & DKA Resuscitation protocols"*) rather than vague algorithmic marketing.
2. **Pedagogical Diagnostic Integrity:** The diagnostic probe provides genuine, high-quality learning feedback and interactive cognitive exploration without being a disguised lead form.
3. **Strict Entitlement Boundary:** Payment never purchases or fakes mastery. Mastery is earned exclusively through validated evidence in cognitive activities.
