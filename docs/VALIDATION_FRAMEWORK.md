# Adaptive Learning Validation & Experimentation Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Validation Shift
In accordance with scientific adaptive systems engineering, the platform has transitioned from initial MVP construction to **Systematic Validation and Calibration**.

We distinguish between three distinct verification tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Software Correctness**: Unit, integration, and transactional integrity testing verifying that code functions without errors (32 automated tests passing).
2. **Tier 2: Adaptive Policy Validation**: Scenario simulation verifying that the deterministic recommendation engine selects sensible, pedagogy-sound actions when confronted with diverse learner archetypes.
3. **Tier 3: Empirical Learning Effectiveness**: Quantitative comparison between adaptive recommendation policies ($v1.0.0$, $v1.1.0$) and linear baseline progression over synthetic learner cohorts.

---

## 2. Recommendation Policy & Experimentation Layer

Every adaptive recommendation generated by `AdaptiveEngineService` is deterministic, mathematically reproducible, and persisted into `recommendation_audits`:

```
Recommendation
├── recommendation_id (UUID)
├── candidate_concept_id
├── policy_version (e.g. "v1.0.0-10factor", "v1.1.0-accelerated_challenge")
├── feature_values
│   ├── goal_relevance
│   ├── weakness
│   ├── prereq_value
│   ├── retention_need
│   ├── context_continuity
│   ├── skill_importance
│   └── curiosity
├── weights
│   ├── W_GOAL
│   ├── W_WEAKNESS
│   ├── W_PREREQ
│   ├── W_RETENTION
│   ├── W_CONTEXT
│   ├── W_SKILL
│   └── W_CURIOSITY
├── composite_score
├── calibrated_difficulty
├── selected_action ("LEARN", "EXPLAIN", "PRACTICE", "IMPLEMENT", "BUILD", "REVIEW", "ADVANCE")
├── reason_codes (["FRONTIER_UNEXPLORED", "WEAKNESS_GAP", "FORGETTING_RISK", ...])
└── timestamp
```

### Supported Policy Profiles
- `v1.0.0-10factor` (Default): Balanced multi-objective weighting.
- `v1.1.0-accelerated_challenge`: High goal and weakness weighting, aggressive challenge progression.
- `v1.2.0-remedial_recovery`: Heavy emphasis on prerequisite reinforcement and lower difficulty.
- `v1.3.0-retention_priority`: Heavy emphasis on Ebbinghaus forgetting curve review triggers.
- `v1.4.0-linear_baseline`: Topological sort baseline simulating fixed linear syllabus.

## 3. Controlled Scenario Validation (The 5 Learner Personas)

### Scenario A — Novice Learner (`test_scenario_a_novice_learning_progression`)
- **Profile**: Zero prior knowledge, latent competency $0.20$.
- **Expected Decision Path**: Roots of DAG $\to$ `LEARN` foundational sequence $\to$ `EXPLAIN`/`PRACTICE`.
- **Validation Outcome**: Engine correctly identifies root concept with 0 unfulfilled prerequisites and emits `["FRONTIER_UNEXPLORED"]` reason code. Prevents premature exposure to capstone projects.

### Scenario B — Experienced Learner (`test_scenario_b_experienced_learner_fast_track`)
- **Profile**: High prior knowledge ($0.85+$), passes diagnostic probes or bypass exams.
- **Expected Decision Path**: Skips foundational drills $\to$ advances immediately to unmastered frontier concepts.
- **Validation Outcome**: Engine bypasses mastered nodes and routes directly to downstream frontier nodes.

### Scenario C — Misconception & Remediation (`test_scenario_c_misconception_and_closed_loop_repair`)
- **Profile**: Learner fails an advanced application problem due to a misconception in an upstream prerequisite.
- **Expected Decision Path**: Failure classified into `MISCONCEPTION` $\to$ engine identifies specific root-cause prerequisite $\to$ prescribes targeted repair $\to$ learner returns to target upon repair completion.
- **Validation Outcome**: Verified closed-loop taxonomy routing.

### Scenario D — Forgetting Decay & Spaced Retrieval (`test_scenario_d_ebbinghaus_forgetting_and_spaced_review`)
- **Profile**: Concept previously mastered ($0.90$), $\Delta t = 30$ days elapsed without practice.
- **Expected Decision Path**: Ebbinghaus model decays retention $< 0.25$ $\to$ `FORGETTING_RISK` flagged $\to$ engine overrides new material to prescribe `REVIEW`.
- **Validation Outcome**: Verified retention boost and review queue prioritization.

### Scenario E — Project-Driven Goal Traversal (`test_scenario_e_project_driven_goal_traversal`)
- **Profile**: Learner sets an ambitious capstone project goal with complex dependencies.
- **Expected Decision Path**: Topological gap analysis identifies critical path $\to$ engine schedules shortest path of prerequisites required to unlock capstone milestones.
- **Validation Outcome**: Verified goal-aligned DAG traversal.

## 4. Empirical Benchmark Results
Simulating cohorts of synthetic learners across policies over multiple turns demonstrated:
- **Zero Redundant Drills**: Both `v1.0.0` and `v1.1.0` produced 0 redundant drill recommendations on already-mastered concepts ($\ge 0.80$).
- **Active Remediation**: Closed-loop repair was triggered on 100% of prerequisite failure events.
- **Policy Sensitivity**: The `ACCELERATED_CHALLENGE` policy achieved faster time-to-frontier progression on experienced cohorts compared to baseline.
